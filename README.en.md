## About

You are **Luna**, the youngest pilot of the kingdom of Aurora. The Star Crystal was shattered by the **Dark Eclipse**, and its 11 fragments of light scattered across the skies. Cross each sky, gather the fragments and collect the gears that transform your ship.

The whole game is **a single HTML file** built with **Three.js** (WebGL). It uses no external images, 3D models or audio files: the scenery, the ships and the **music are all generated by code**.

## Highlights

- **14 levels:** 11 in the campaign plus 3 bonus worlds unlocked after beating the game.
- **15 procedural tracks** (14 levels + the menu theme) that speed up in the middle and at the end of each level.
- **Dynamic weather:** rain, snow, lightning, meteors, embers, petals, northern lights and bubbles.
- **3 boss fights:** two mini-bosses (levels 5 and 8) and the final boss, Eclipse.
- **6 ships** with unique abilities, each with **12 forms** that evolve as you collect gears.
- **Shop, stars per level, daily missions and Endless mode.**
- **Leaderboards** for the campaign and Endless mode.
- **Accessibility:** photosensitivity warning, a reduce-flashes option, 3 languages, separate volume sliders and optional vibration.

## Controls

| Action | Desktop | Mobile |
|---|---|---|
| Move the ship | Mouse, arrow keys or WASD | Drag your finger |
| Dash (Comet ship) | Space bar | DASH button |
| Pause | `P`, `Esc` or the **II** button | **II** button |
| Music on/off | `M` | Options |

**Goal:** survive until the progress bar fills and fly through the rings. Gold = 10 points, blue = 25, pink = 50, and the rainbow ring gives 100 points and +1 life. Grab the 2 green gears in each level to evolve your ship. In boss fights, rings damage the enemy.

## Ships

| Ship | Price (★) | Ability |
|---|---|---|
| Aurora | Free | +0.4 s invincibility after a hit |
| Falcon | 500 | Much faster steering |
| Manta | 4,000 | Passive magnet that pulls rings |
| Comet | 11,000 | Invincible dash (12 s cooldown) |
| Phoenix | 24,000 | Revives once per level |
| Dragon | 50,000 | Fire shield (absorbs 1 hit every 25 s) |

## Tech

- **HTML5 + JavaScript** (no build step, no framework)
- **[Three.js r128](https://threejs.org/)** for 3D (WebGL)
- **Web Audio API** for the sequencer and synths behind the music
- **localStorage** for progress, shop, missions and settings

## Run locally

There is nothing to install. Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
# open http://localhost:8000
```
